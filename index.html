About Us

MyDuka is a powerful, easy-to-use desktop application for tracking inventory, managing products, and generating reports - all in one place.

It provides real-time tracking of your inventory levels with customizable alerts, advanced data management tools, and powerful visualization capabilities to optimize your inventory operations.

Simple to input inventory. Simple to use. Simple to customize.

Who Can Use MyDuka

Duka Owners

Manage your retail shop — track stock, sales, expiry and daily profit effortlessly.

Supermarkets & Mini-Marts

Oversee thousands of products — barcode scanning, low-stock alerts & reporting.

Boutiques & Cosmetics Shops

Track sizes, colors and variants — know what is selling and what to restock.

Hardware & Spare Parts

Manage tools, materials and equipment with buying price vs selling price margin.

Restaurants & Cafes

Control ingredients and supplies — prevent theft, waste and stock-outs.

Wholesalers & Distributors

Bulk stock in/out, supplier debts, and customer credit — all in one dashboard.
